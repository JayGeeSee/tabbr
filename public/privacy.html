TABBR privacy policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

In short: TABBR keeps your data on your phone. There are no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking. The developer never receives your training data or your location.

What TABBR stores, and where

Your profile (name or callsign, goal, level, units, body weight), your logged sessions, GPS routes, XP, badges and settings are stored only on your device. They are not sent to TABBR or anyone else.

If you use Save backup, TABBR creates a file and hands it to you. Where you keep it (for example iCloud Drive) is your choice.

Location

TABBR uses your location only while you are tracking a tab with GPS, to measure distance, pace, splits and your route. In the iPhone and Android apps, tracking continues while your screen is locked, until you pause or finish the tab. On Android a notification is shown whenever this is happening. Location is never collected at any other time and never leaves your device.

Maps

When you view a session's route map, the map images are downloaded from OpenStreetMap's tile servers. Like any website, those servers receive your device's IP address and the map area being viewed. Your route itself is not sent; it is drawn on your device. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy.

Hosting

The web version of TABBR is hosted on Vercel, which keeps standard server logs (such as IP addresses) when the app's files are downloaded. The iPhone and Android apps include their files and do not need to download them.

Children

TABBR is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Delete individual sessions in the app, or remove all of your data by deleting the app (or, for the web version, clearing the site's data in your browser).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
